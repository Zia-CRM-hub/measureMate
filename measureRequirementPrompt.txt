MeasureMate™ - AI-Native SaaS Platform for Tailors, Boutiques & Custom Fashion Businesses
Objective

Design and build a modern, enterprise-grade, multi-tenant SaaS platform called MeasureMate™ that enables tailors, boutiques, fashion designers, alteration centers, uniform providers, and custom apparel businesses to efficiently manage customer measurements, orders, production, payments, and business operations.

The platform must deliver an exceptional user experience for both small tailor shops and large boutique chains, while remaining affordable, scalable, mobile-first, and built entirely using open-source technologies.

Vision Statement

MeasureMate should become the "Shopify for Tailors and Boutique Businesses."

A single platform where fashion businesses can:

Manage customers
Store measurements permanently
Track family members and relatives
Create stitching orders
Track production status
Manage payments
Generate invoices
Maintain customer history
Operate across multiple branches
Access data through web and mobile applications

The platform must eliminate physical measurement books, paper records, and manual follow-ups.
